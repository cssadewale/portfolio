CBT Pro (CBT.ng)

"Built by a tutor, for tutors. Not with web dev skills — with clear thinking and AI leverage."

ProblemNigerian teachers had no free, reliable digital exam tool. Paper CBT was slow, error-prone, and inaccessible to ordinary teachers without technical backgrounds.
SolutionFull-stack CBT platform: 7-column CSV question upload, timed sessions with auto-submit, per-student randomisation, anti-cheat tab-switch detection, flag-for-review, auto-marking, certificates with WhatsApp share, 4-tab analytics dashboard, optional Gemini AI explanations.
ImpactLive with real secondary school students today. Built entirely on an Android tablet using Acode editor — zero budget, zero compromise. This is what AI-Augmented Solutions Development looks like in practice.
Platform Features
  • CSV question upload — 7-column format with optional AI explanations. No manual entry.
  • Timed exam sessions with countdown timer and auto-submit
  • Per-student question and option randomisation (anti-copy)
  • Anti-cheat tab-switch detection with configurable tolerance
  • Flag-for-review during exam — revisit before submitting
  • Auto-marking + result certificate with WhatsApp share
  • 4-tab analytics: Overview · Question Analysis · Student Comparison · Trends
  • Optional Gemini API AI explanation per question
