Teaching Philosophy

"Learning Deliberately. Teaching Authentically."

I believe every student who is struggling has a gap — not a deficiency. My job is to find that gap, close it, and make the subject make sense. I do not teach to a class. I teach to individuals who happen to be in the same room. Over 15 years, I have taught Mathematics, Further Mathematics, Physics, Chemistry, and Computer Science across nursery, primary, and secondary levels in Lagos and Ogun State.

I bring data science and technology into how I teach — tracking performance analytically, identifying knowledge gaps with precision, and personalising learning pathways. I hold a B.Sc.(Ed) in Computer Science Education from Lagos State University (2023), and I believe that the future of education belongs to teachers who are not afraid to learn new things. That belief is not theoretical. It is how I live.
